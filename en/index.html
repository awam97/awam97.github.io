What I can fix for you

The problems people bring me most often, and how I solve them.

"The site is slow and loads badly on phones."

I compress images, sort out caching and heavy plugins, and change hosting if needed. I measure with PageSpeed before and after, so you can see the difference.

"Nobody updates the site, and I'm afraid it will break."

Monthly maintenance: backups, updates tested on a copy first, security checks and bug fixes.

"I have a Figma design and need someone to turn it into a site."

I build it in WordPress, with Elementor or a custom theme, true to the design and easy for the client to edit.

"I want to sell online."

A WooCommerce shop with products, payments, shipping and order emails, plus small plugins when something specific is needed.

"Enquiries from the site get lost, or I copy them by hand."

I connect the site's form to the CRM, Google Sheets and email with Make: every enquiry is saved, you get an alert and the client gets a reply straight away.

"Spreadsheets and email aren't enough to run the work any more."

I build a custom system in PHP and CodeIgniter: logins with users and roles, records, PDF reports, and a connected app if needed.

"The site can't be found on Google."

I fix the technical SEO: titles, structure, sitemap, speed, Search Console and Analytics, so you know where visits come from.

"Our agency has more work than developers."

I join your workflow remotely, per project or for a few days a month, using your tools and your rules.
